# Video types

Budgets assume about 2.5 spoken words/second (150 wpm), which is a natural, unhurried promo pace. Leave 10–20% of runtime without VO for visual breathing room and the CTA.

| Type | Runtime | Scenes | VO words | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teaser | 5–10s | 3–5 | 0–25 | Curiosity. Make them click. |
| Promo | 15–45s | 5–9 | 30–100 | Desire. Hook, then problem, solution, CTA. |
| Overview | 60–300s | 8–25 | 130–650 | Understanding. What it does and why it matters. |
| Tutorial (each) | 60–600s | 6–40 | 130–1300 | Competence. The viewer can do the task. |

## Teaser (5–10s)
- **Structure:** hook (0–2s), then 2–3 fast solution flashes (screenshots or motivating images, about 1.5s each), then logo + CTA/date (last 1.5–2s).
- The VO is a single line, or none at all, with music and kinetic text carrying it. Cut on the beat.
- **Don't** explain features. Show outcomes. One bold claim.
- Works muted. On-screen text has to carry the message.

## Promo (15–45s): hook / problem / solution
1. **Hook (0–3s):** a pattern interrupt, i.e. a provocative question, a surprising stat, or the pain shown visually.
2. **Problem (about 20%):** name the pain in the audience's words. One scene, two at most.
3. **Solution (about 50%):** the product as the turn. 2–4 features, each one scene, phrased as benefits ("Find any file in a second", not "Indexed search").
4. **Proof (optional):** a logo strip, a metric, or a quote.
5. **CTA (last 3–5s):** one action, the URL on screen, and the logo.

## Overview (60–300s)
- **Hook (≤5s)**, then **value prop (≤15s)**, then a **feature tour**, then a **recap and CTA**.
- **Feature tour:** one scene per feature, in workflow order (how a user would actually move through the app), not menu order.
- Each feature beat follows "what it is, what it shows, why it matters". Zoom into the relevant UI region, because full-screen screenshots are unreadable at video size.
- Use chapter title cards every 60–90s on longer overviews.

## Tutorial series (each 60–600s)
- **One task per episode.** The title is a verb phrase: "Create your first project", "Invite your team".
- **Per episode:**
  1. **Goal (≤10s):** what you'll be able to do.
  2. **Prerequisites:** link the earlier episodes.
  3. **Steps:** one scene per step, with the cursor/click highlighted and the step number on screen.
  4. **Recap:** a bulleted summary.
  5. **Next:** name the next episode.
- **Series order:** onboarding/setup first, then core daily tasks, then advanced/admin. Each episode stands alone, with no "as we saw last time" that is required for comprehension.
- Prefer real screen recordings over stills for multi-step UI flows. Use stills plus zoom for single-screen explanations.
- Keep the same intro/outro, voice, and music across the series. Keep the intro ≤3s, since people skip repeated intros.
- **Deliverables:** `out/NN-<slug>.mp4` plus `out/index.md` (order, titles, durations, prerequisites).
