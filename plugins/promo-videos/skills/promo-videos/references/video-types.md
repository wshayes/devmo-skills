# Video types

| Type | Runtime | Scenes | VO pace | VO words | Goal | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teaser | 5–10s | 3–5 | (optional) | 0–20 | Curiosity. Make them click. | [teaser.md](guidelines/teaser.md) |
| Promo | 15–45s | 5–9 | ~2.5 w/s | 30–100 | Desire. Hook, then problem, solution, CTA. | [promo.md](guidelines/promo.md) |
| Overview | 60–300s | 8–25 | ~2.3 w/s | 130–650 | Understanding. What it does and why it matters. | [overview.md](guidelines/overview.md) |
| Tutorial (each) | 60–600s | 6–40 | ~2.1 w/s | 120–1200 | Competence. The viewer can do the task. | [tutorial.md](guidelines/tutorial.md) |

Leave 10–20% of runtime without VO for visual breathing room, step results, and the CTA hold.

[guidelines/common.md](guidelines/common.md) applies to every type: message, picture, on-screen text, audio mix and loudness, accessibility, and delivery formats.

## Choosing a type

| If the user wants to… | Make |
|---|---|
| tease a launch, get a social click, or a bumper | Teaser |
| sell the value fast for ads, social, or a landing-page hero | Promo |
| explain the product to evaluators for a website, sales, or YouTube | Overview |
| teach people to use features for onboarding, docs, or an LMS | Tutorial series |

A launch often wants several: teaser, then promo, then overview, all from one asset capture with a shared voice and music style. Offer this, but build one video at a time.
