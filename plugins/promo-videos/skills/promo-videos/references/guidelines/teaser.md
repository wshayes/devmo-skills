# Teaser guidelines (5–10s)

**Job:** create curiosity and earn one action, which is a click, a follow or a "remember the date". A teaser doesn't explain anything. It promises.
**Success test:** a stranger who sees it muted, once, can say what the product is *about* and wants to know more.

## Structure

| Beat | Time (8s example) | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–1.5s | The boldest visual or line. No logo, no fade-in from black. |
| Flashes | 1.5–6s | 2–3 solution glimpses (an outcome screenshot, a motivating image, or a single UI moment), about 1.5s each. |
| Reveal + CTA | 6–8s | Logo, a ≤5-word tagline, and the CTA (URL, "Coming <date>", or "Watch the demo"). |

## Script
- **VO is optional.** If you use it, keep it to one line of ≤20 words, or two short lines split hook and reveal. Music plus kinetic text often works better.
- **Hook patterns:**
  - a provocative question ("What if month-end took an hour?")
  - a bold claim ("Your spreadsheet just retired.")
  - a striking before/after visual
- **Don't name features.** Name the feeling or the outcome.
- **Leave a gap.** The teaser should leave the viewer asking "how?". The promo or overview answers it.

## Visuals
- **Every frame must work as a thumbnail.** Lead with the single most impressive UI moment, not the login screen.
- Use tight crops and zooms on the one interesting detail of each screenshot. Avoid full dashboards.
- **Fast cuts, but readable.** Use ≥1.2s per shot, ≤3 flashes per second (WCAG), and hold the end card at least 2s.
- **2.5D tilt and push-in suit hero shots.** Keep the same move direction throughout for momentum.
- **Loopable ending.** On autoplaying social feeds, let the last frame flow back into the first. Use the same background color or a matching cut.

## Audio
- Pick music with a clear hit or drop, and cut the flashes on the beat. Generate the ElevenLabs Music bed with a "hit at 1.5s" or "drop at <reveal time>" prompt, or choose a punchy GarageBand beat loop.
- End on a sting or a clean button. No fade-out tail on a 7s video.

## Formats
- Make 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, plus 1:1 for LinkedIn and X feeds. 16:9 is only for website heroes and YouTube bumpers.
- Burn in all text. Assume muted playback.

## Pitfalls
- Opening on the logo, which viewers scroll past before it means anything.
- Cramming 5+ features into it. That's a promo.
- Tiny UI text that can't be read on a phone.
- An ending without a CTA, or with three CTAs.

## Pre-render checklist
- [ ] Runtime 5–10s, and the hook lands within 1.5s.
- [ ] It works muted, and all the meaning is in the visuals and text.
- [ ] ≤3 cuts per second, and the end card holds ≥2s.
- [ ] One CTA, readable on a phone.
- [ ] Made in the target aspect ratio(s).
